# Initial Risk Register

Scale: likelihood and impact are Low, Medium, High, or Critical. Owners are logical roles, not external assignments.

| ID | Risk | L / I | Controls and evidence | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UX-01 | Unmodified arrows conflict with native scrolling and screen-reader browse/Quick Nav commands. | High / High | Narrow late cancellation, native focus, candid claims, AT/manual tests, evaluate explicit mode. | Accessibility specialist | Open; user decision |
| DET-01 | Google DOM drift selects ads, shopping, navigation, or unrelated links. | High / High | One versioned adapter requires a narrow root/block/organic/title structure and rejects known rich-module signals; synthetic adversarial fixtures and action-time revalidation are present. | Extension engineer | Reduced for private test; real Chrome evidence open |
| A11Y-01 | Indicator or focus semantics are imperceivable or misleading. | Medium / High | Real native-anchor focus, no faux ARIA selection, 2 px/3:1 target, zoom/forced-color/AT tests. | Accessibility specialist | Open pending evidence |
| KEY-01 | Key handling disrupts editing, IME, widgets, modifiers, Tab, or native activation. | Medium / High | Composed-path plus root/ancestor guards, composition/defaultPrevented checks, exhaustive key matrix, no Tab/Enter interception. | Test engineer | Static controls corrected; browser/AT evidence pending |
| DYN-01 | Selected result is removed/replaced and focus jumps or stale state activates. | High / Medium | Revalidate each action; preserve only the same connected eligible node; otherwise clear without focus transfer. | Extension engineer | Static control present; browser evidence pending |

Review at every stage exit and after any finding, scope change, policy update, or failed gate. Risks are closed only with evidence; accepted risk requires the applicable human authority.
