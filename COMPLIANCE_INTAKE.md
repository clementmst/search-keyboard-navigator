# Minimal Compliance Intake

This questionnaire is the dedicated compliance reviewer's minimal intake for the operator to ask the user at the relevant gate. Project agents do not ask the user directly. It requests only facts that materially change legal or platform-policy obligations, and it is not a substitute for qualified legal advice.

Answered facts are recorded in [COMPLIANCE.md](COMPLIANCE.md) and [compliance/register.yaml](compliance/register.yaml). Ask only these remaining questions.

## Required now before private testing

1. **Will anyone besides you install the private build, in which countries, and will testing use real personal/account-based searches, screenshots, logs, or shared builds?** External testers or captured personal Search material can change distribution, privacy, confidentiality, rights, and evidence-handling obligations.

## Required before public distribution

No additional publisher-country, launch-market, or audience question remains: the publisher is an individual in France, worldwide availability is intended, and the general-public product will not be specifically directed at children under 16.

The future direction is answered: optional privacy-minimized first-party analytics, deferred behind a separate complete proposal. No telemetry question blocks private testing, and nothing is collected unless separately authorized.

## Only needed if monetization, data collection, or marketing is introduced

1. **When the agreed post-live donation task begins, which donation provider will be used and where will payments be processed and reported by the French publisher?** Provider and payment locations determine tax, identity, consumer, privacy, Store-placement, transfer, and recordkeeping obligations.
2. **If telemetry or marketing is reconsidered, what exact data or claims are proposed, for what purpose, audience, countries, recipients, retention, and channels?** Those details determine whether the proposal is lawful, proportionate, consented, secure, permitted by Store policy, and supported by evidence.

## Operator handling

Record answers with date and scope in the compliance register, but do not store credentials, private queries, personal identifiers, home addresses, or agreement-acceptance material in the repository. Escalate only the resulting material choices, legal-advice needs, or release blockers.
