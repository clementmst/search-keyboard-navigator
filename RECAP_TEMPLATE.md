# Operator final interaction-close template

Use this structure only at the end of the operator chat's final user-facing message for an interaction. Keep intermediate commentary natural and concise; do not repeat this structure after internal steps. Project tasks and individual agents do not use this template for their own interfaces, internal reviews, briefs, or technical reports.

The operator chat is the sole normal human interface. Execution/review agents send findings and choices to the operator; the user is not asked to coordinate agents or read technical discussions. Surface through the operator only material product choices, safety/privacy/security concerns, new permissions or credentials, costs/dependencies, irreversible external actions, or human-only browser/accessibility validation.

## What we achieved

State the outcome in short, plain language. Do not turn static evidence into a browser or real-user claim.

## Proposed sequence

Provide an ordered workplan of next actions and recommendations. If no work remains, say so as item 1 rather than inventing follow-up work. Do not imply authorization that has not been granted.
